Why it finds deals other tools miss

Sticker prices lie. Pack sizes, Buy1-Get1 offers and per-unit maths decide what's actually cheap.

⚖️True unit prices

Every result is normalised to ৳/kg, ৳/L or ৳/piece — including multipacks like “2 × 400gm” and “5gm 24 pcs”.

🎁Buy1-Get1 aware

A 180ml B1G1 shampoo is really 360ml in your bag. The extension counts it, tags it ×2, and ranks it by what you actually pay per litre.

🏆Explained best deal

The winner is crowned with the reason — “2× bigger for ৳21 more, 45% cheaper per litre” — plus the cheapest same-size option pinned next to it.

৳Compare from any page

A small ৳ button appears next to product titles on shop pages; or select any product name anywhere → right-click → Compare BD prices.

🛒One cart, every store

Build a single cart, then let it fill each store's real cart through your own session. Checkout and payment stay on the store's site, in your hands.

⭐Price watch

Star any product; it's re-checked daily and you get a notification the moment the price drops below what you starred it at.

🧭Category smart

Knows groceries from electronics, auto-detects which one you're searching, and never opens pointless tabs — stores are queried one at a time, politely.

🌏English + বাংলা

One tap switches the entire interface, explanations included. Location-aware too: it follows your last-visited pandamart and your Meena Bazar branch.

Install in under a minute

It's a standard unpacked Chrome extension — no account, no build tools.

  1. Download & unzip. Grab bd-price-compare.zip and extract it anywhere (keep the folder).
  2. Enable Developer mode. Open chrome://extensions and flip the “Developer mode” toggle in the top-right corner.
  3. Load unpacked. Click “Load unpacked”, choose the extracted bd-price-compare folder, then pin the ৳ icon. Done — search your first product.

Works on Chrome, Edge, Brave and any Chromium browser. To update later: download the new zip over the old folder (or git pull) and hit ↻ on the extensions page.

Private by design

🔒No server, no tracking

🤝Polite to the stores

Frequently asked questions

Which Bangladeshi stores does it search?

Groceries: Shwapno, Meena Bazar, Pandamart (foodpanda), Chaldal. Electronics: Star Tech, Ryans, Gadget & Gear, Pickaboo, Techland, Sumash Tech, Global Brand. Both: Daraz and Othoba. You choose which ones in Settings; the major three per category are on by default.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Completely free and open source. No account, no sign-up, no server — it uses the store logins already in your browser.

How does it pick the “best deal”?

Lowest unit price among comparable products — ৳ per kg or litre — with pack sizes and Buy1-Get1 offers counted in. When a bigger pack wins despite a higher sticker price, it tells you why and pins the cheapest same-size option right below.

Can it place orders for me?

It fills each store's real cart for you using your own session, then hands over. Login, checkout and payment always happen on the store's own website, by you.

Why isn't it on the Chrome Web Store?

It's a personal open-source project installed as an unpacked extension — the download-and-load takes about a minute. A Web Store release may come later.
